Privacy & ordering

We use an essential signed cookie to recognise this browser’s orders. Your cart is stored on this device. Customers do not need a ChatGPT account or staff password.

Order information

Orders include your selected table, items, quantities, optional kitchen note, and preparation history. Authorised café staff can view and manage them. Do not enter payment details or sensitive personal information in a kitchen note. Payment is handled at the café.

Orders are stored with Netlify Blobs. A hashed network identifier is used to limit repeated requests; raw IP addresses are not saved in our order records. Staff sign-in attempts are rate-limited using a hashed network identifier.

Cookies and access

The customer cookie lasts up to 30 days; staff sessions last up to eight hours. Clearing cookies, changing browser, or changing device means earlier customer orders may no longer be visible to you. Only staff can see orders across devices. Contact the café for help with an earlier order.

Photos and external services

Some real food photographs are representative category images, not Happy Days dishes. Credits identify their sources. External photo requests may disclose your IP address to the image host. Google Maps loads only when requested. This version has no analytics trackers.

Questions and deletion requests

Contact the café on +91 85608 45571 about order information or a deletion request. Staff manage retained orders; automatic deletion is not enabled.
