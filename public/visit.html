GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Is the café vegetarian?+

Yes, Happy Days is listed as a vegetarian café and restaurant.

Can I book a table?+

Call us with your preferred date, time and group size. Your table is confirmed directly by the café, subject to availability.

Can I order takeaway?+

Call to ask about available dishes, pricing and pickup time. You can also check the café’s Zomato listing for online options.

Where can I see the current menu?+

Our menu page includes selected items from the café menu. For the full current selection and prices, call the café or check Zomato.

What about allergies or a larger group?+

Please speak to us before visiting. We can discuss food ingredients, seating and any arrangements you need.
